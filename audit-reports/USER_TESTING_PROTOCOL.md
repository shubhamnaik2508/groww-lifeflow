# Target-user testing protocol and findings template

**Status: no genuine target-user sessions have been conducted.** Nothing below is a result. Fill in the template only after real sessions.

## Eligibility (screen every participant)

- Aged 22–26.
- Salaried, in the first 12 months of their first job.
- Lives or works in a Tier 2 Indian city.
- Started their first mutual-fund SIP from salary income.
- Not a finance professional; not on the project team.

Record informed consent: purpose, that no real financial data is used, notes kept anonymously, and the right to stop at any time.

## Session (30 minutes, think-aloud, phone preferred)

1. **Context (3 min):** "Tell me about a month when money felt tight after you started your SIP."
2. **Tasks.** Observe; don't help unless they're stuck for more than 60 s:
   1. Enter an illustrative monthly picture.
   2. Explore what happens if your salary is late by a week and your SIP is due.
   3. Ask the companion what you should check.
   4. Find out whether anything here is verified against your real account.
   5. Compare two situations you explored.
3. **Comprehension check:** "What does ₹4,000 mean here? Is it your bank balance?" and "What would you do next?"
4. **AI vs rule-based (counterbalanced):** show both explanations for the same scenario. Ask which is clearer and more trustworthy, and why.
5. **Debrief (5 min):** the most and least useful parts; would you use this before your SIP date?

## Measures

- Task completion: yes, no or assisted.
- Time on task.
- Errors.
- Confidence rating (1–5) before and after.
- Clarity rating (1–5) for the AI and rule-based explanations.
- Exact non-sensitive quotes.
