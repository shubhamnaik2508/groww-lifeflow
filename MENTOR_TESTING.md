# Mentor testing checklist

Do not record sensitive information. Use illustrative inputs. Recruit at least three consenting target users and record observations separately for each. No sessions have been conducted yet.

1. Enter an illustrative salary, expenses, SIP and date.
2. Simulate an unexpected expense and inspect the before/after estimate.
3. Compare two saved scenarios and explain the assumptions.
4. Simulate a delayed salary and identify what remains unknown.
5. Load browser AI (note download time and device), ask a contextual question, and record whether the answer was AI-generated (all checks passed) or the labelled rule-based fallback, and which check failed.
6. Open the SEBI source and assess whether it supports the claim shown.
7. Inspect quality information; identify which checks actually ran.
8. Ask for a fund recommendation and inspect the fallback.
9. Ask a bank-specific question and inspect the uncertainty.
10. Navigate all destinations on mobile and desktop and try reset.

For each task record completion (yes/no/assisted), confusion points, errors, exact non-sensitive feedback, and changes made in response. Record participant consent and date. Do not enter results before sessions occur.
